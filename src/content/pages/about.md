Aloha mai kākou! I’m Samantha Mallari, a software engineer based in Honolulu.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science in Spring 2025 and my M.S. in Spring 2026 from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa through the combined Bachelor’s and Master’s pathway.

My interests include embedded systems, computer networks, machine learning, robotics, and space. I work on satellite flight software at the Hawaiʻi Space Flight Laboratory.

This site is a place for my work, research, and writing. More to come.
